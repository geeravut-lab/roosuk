Accessed: 2026-10 (research date 2026-10-05). Researcher: Claude (market-research subagent).

## 0. Method and honesty notes (read first)

- **WebFetch was blocked by the sandbox egress proxy for every domain tried** (hdmall.co.th, hfocus.org, thestandard.co, apps.apple.com, bangkokpost.com, nationthailand.com, true.th, lab.morroo.com, thestorythailand.com). All facts below therefore come from **WebSearch result summaries** (secondary snippets of the cited pages), not from reading the full pages. Treat numbers as "as reported by the cited page"; anything I could not see is marked **unverified**.
- App-store ratings/download figures are snapshots from search snippets and may be stale; verify before quoting externally.
- Prices: only quoted where a source stated them. Thai-language UI is stated only where the source/product is clearly Thai-first (Thai-made, Thai store listing) — otherwise "unverified".
- "Has / Lacks" columns are mapped to RooSuk's feature list, based on what the sources say. "Lacks" means "not mentioned in sources I saw", not proof of absence.

## 1. Executive summary (what matters for RooSuk)

1. **There is no single Thai product that matches RooSuk's full bundle** (daily check-in + Health Score + streak + food-photo scan + lab-scan with trend + Ask-AI over own data + document vault + Health Passport + family + challenges/credit + marketplace + corporate + PDPA granular consent + PromptPay). Competitors cover _slices_:
   - Food photo calorie scan: **Kalguroo** (1M+ downloads, 4.7–4.8 rating), **NubCal**, **Fastcal**, **Gindee Food AI** (LINE bot by CARIVA), **HDmall** scanner.
   - Lab-result AI explanation: **HDmall** (launched 18 Feb 2026), **Lab.morroo** (Thai-first, 3 free reads/month, trend + PDF export), **Kantesti** (foreign, multi-language).
   - Records/booking: **Mor Prom Super App** (national, 30M+ historical users), **Paotang Health Wallet**, hospital apps (BDMS My B+/BeDee, Bumrungrad, Samitivej Well, Siriraj, RAMA, PR9).
   - Gamified wellness: **Mor Prom x True "10,000 steps" credits**, **AIA+/Vitality**, **Muang Thai Life Fit Rewards**, **FWD Omne**, **Well by Samitivej coins**.
2. **Closest direct threats:** (a) **HDmall** — already has AI calorie scan + AI blood-test reading + Jib AI chatbot + marketplace + installment payments, free app; (b) **Kalguroo** — proven Thai consumer traction and a planned "personalised AI coach"; (c) **Lab.morroo / Morroo Advice** — Thai-first lab scan + free AI chat; (d) **Mor Prom Super App** — free, national, with True partnership for AI tips and walking credits.
3. **Whitespace for RooSuk:** a single _cross-source_ consumer view (food + labs + wearables + documents) with _granular PDPA consent_, a _shareable Health Passport / pre-doctor brief_, _family sharing_, and a _low price_ (49/89 THB) — none of the above sources showed all of these together. Hospital apps are siloed per hospital; Mor Prom is institution-record-centric; calorie apps ignore labs.
4. **Pricing context:** Kalguroo's store listings show US$3.99–9.99/mo and US$59.99/yr; NubCal ~999 THB/yr; Cal AI ~US$9.99/mo or ~US$29.99/yr (third-party). RooSuk's 49/89 THB per month sits well below these, while the national app and HDmall features are free.
5. **Regulatory watch-outs:** health data is PDPA "sensitive" (explicit, separate consent; fines now actually issued); Thai FDA SaMD/AI guidance (Jun/Oct 2024) treats diagnostic/CDS software as potentially a medical device but excludes simple record storage and BMI-type calculators; new Thai FDA food-advertising notification effective 28 Jul 2026 affects the supplement marketplace.

## 3. Per-product notes

### 3.1 Government / national

**หมอพร้อม / Mohpromt (Super App)** — Owner: Ministry of Public Health (MOPH). Began 2021 as the COVID vaccine/certificate app; upgraded in 2022 into a 12-feature health app (partners named by sources: Kasikornbank, INET, Thai Red Cross, SCG Logistics), and a "Super App" rollout by health region beginning with Region 6 (Chonburi), Region 4 etc., with an official launch date reported as 12 Jan 2026. Features: online appointments, telemedicine, digital medical certificate, e-payment, coverage/insurance claim checks, treatment history, drug allergies, lab results, queue tracking, Caregiver ID, Health ID registration, health and mental-health self-assessment, pregnancy record. Linked to Health Link (8,700+ non-MOPH facilities, 1,500+ in Bangkok) and ~15,000 service units/drugstores (reported). Scale: "30M+ users" is the COVID-era figure; Super App active usage **unverified**. Privacy: sources say PDPA-aligned, two-tier data access, MOU + DSA with each connected entity, Health ID identity check. AI: **March 2026 MOPH–True MoU** to use consented health data for personalised tips/early illness prediction; True co-runs a "10,000 steps" campaign (10,000 steps = 5 Mohpromt Credits, bonus for 7-day streak; redeemable at True shops/TrueCoffee, e.g. up to THB 1,500 handset discount, THB 35 coffee discount). Strengths: free, trusted, institution-grade records, national reach. Weaknesses: Bangkok Post opinion piece criticises "another health app" redundancy (50+ MOPH apps plus hospital apps) and siloed data culture; no food/lab-AI/wearable-trend seen; rollout region by region. Sources: S1–S8, S34, S35.

**เป๋าตัง (Paotang) — "กระเป๋าสุขภาพ" Health Wallet** — Owner: Krungthai Bank (the app) with NHSO (บัตรทอง) / SSO (ประกันสังคม). Check entitlements, book free checkups/health-promotion services, pharmacy-based minor-illness service, online doctor consult, and view PHR/HIE/Health Link data; NHSO promoted it again in May 2026. No AI explanation, no logging. Mass reach through the wallet. Sources: S9–S12.

**NHSO / Health Link / ทางรัฐ** — Health Link (BDI + NHSO + DGA) connects ~20,000 facilities and exposes treatment history in the "ทางรัฐ" government app; NHSO also uses AI to check claims. Infrastructure rather than consumer wellness. Source: S13.

**MyHealth Thailand (NECTEC)** — Health-record/checkup tracker with age-appropriate checkup recommendations, risk assessment and doctor communication; one of the oldest Thai PHR apps. Appears dated (no AI). Sources: S14, S15.

**ThaiHealth (สสส.) apps** — "Persona Health" (alcohol-abstinence campaign companion, daily actions) and "ไทยสุข/ThaiSook" (food, exercise, smoking, alcohol behaviours); smoking-cessation tools (U-Refer, SMART อสม.). Campaign-oriented, free, no lab/records. Sources: S16, S17.

### 3.2 Hospital group apps

**BDMS: My B+ / BDMS Health Passport / BeDee** — My B+: telemedicine, self-service appointments and check-ups, health records (lab results, COVID results, medicines, appointment history), family members' records, health articles, packages, e-payment. BDMS Health Passport: view records, labs, find doctors, appointments. **BeDee** (launched with CARIVA as co-developer): tele-consultation, telepharmacy, Health Mall, "Health Passport" consolidating corporate/BDMS patient checkup results, insurance OPD benefits; ISO 27001:2022 and ISO 27799:2016 claimed. BDMS invested in CARIVA (Apr 2024) for AI personalised diagnosis; BDMS–FWD "Smart Discharge" for 23 hospitals. Strength: largest hospital network, data from real tests. Weakness: tied to BDMS facilities; consumer AI explanation features not seen. Sources: S18–S24.

**Bumrungrad app** — Doctor search, appointments, packages, "My Clinical Reports" (vitals, health screening, labs, DNA reports), loyalty card, 24/7 telemedicine, medication tracking. Google Play 50K+ downloads, 4.5★ (257 reviews). Hospital AI used clinically (X-ray, mammography, blood analysis) rather than in the consumer app (consumer AI **unverified**). Sources: S25–S27.

**Well by Samitivej / Samitivej Plus** — The most RooSuk-like hospital app: AI-based personalised "Food" and "Fit" plans, smartwatch connection, medical records (appointments, video consult, check-up reports, payments, medicine delivery), daily challenges with Coins. iOS 4.6★ (200 ratings) 62K+ downloads, Android 100K+. Samitivej One ID unifies login; Samitivej also sells AI-assisted check-up packages (THB 4,600, 16 items, 7 risk conditions). Weakness: Samitivej-patient centric; no food-photo scan or cross-provider lab trend shown. Sources: S28–S31.

**PR9 Care (Praram 9)** — records, real-time appointments, pre-visit note, queue, Face/Touch ID. **Thonburi Hospital app** exists (Thonburi Healthcare Group) — features **unverified**. **Vibhavadi** — no dedicated app found. Source: S32.

**Siriraj Connect** — queue tracking, appointment info, pre-authorisation document upload, entitlement check. 411K+ downloads but 3.2★ on Google Play (reported) — evidence of weak UX satisfaction in hospital apps. Source: S33.

**RAMA App (Ramathibodi)** — appointments, records, lab results, pharmacy status, mobile payment, document upload (max 10 files, 5 MB each) for appointments. (Note: "RAMA Channel" is the hospital's media channel, not the app.) Source: S36, S37.

### 3.3 Telemedicine / platforms / retail

**Doctor Anywhere Thailand (incl. acquired Raksa)** — Owner: Doctor Anywhere (Singapore); Raksa acquired Nov 2021 (~1M users added). Video consults ~500 THB (reports 300–500), GP and specialist, mental wellness, screening, vaccinations, medicine delivery (free within 90 min in 27 provinces), in-app referrals/MC/reports; corporate "DA Care" outpatient cover and corporate screening. No self-tracking/AI-over-your-data. Sources: S38–S41.

**HDmall (HD, Thailand)** — Thai health marketplace (booking checkups, surgeries, beauty, medicines, installments). Reported 400K paying customers (TH+ID), 30,000+ SKUs, 2,500+ providers; funding total ~US$18M incl. Merck (MSD) investment (Feb 2025). AI: **Jib AI** chatbot (about 60% of customer interactions handled by AI agents), **AI calorie scanner** (photo -> calories/protein/fat/carbs/sugar; free in app), **AI blood-test reading** (announced 18 Feb 2026: photo/file -> auto-extract values -> plain-Thai explanation + personalised suggestions), skin-age analysis, period tracking, pharmacist chat; Jib OS for clinics. The only mainstream Thai app combining food scan + lab-read + chat + commerce. Weaknesses/unknowns: trend-over-time, document vault, family, Passport, wearable import, granular consent — **not seen**; commercial incentive is to sell packages. Sources: S42–S48.

**Ooca** — Founded 2017; video sessions with psychiatrists/psychologists (30 min ~THB 1,000–1,500), anonymous registration, 130K+ registered users, 100+ professionals, corporate programmes (76K employees at Bank of Thailand, Chevron, SCG Chemicals, KBank). Relevant as the Thai corporate-wellness benchmark. Sources: S49, S50.

**MorDee / True Health (True Digital)** — Telemedicine with 500+ doctors, 20 specialities, consult summaries, e-prescription delivery, insurance claim in-app; True Health Corner kiosks in Lotus/Makro (18 sites, reported); partnerships with insurers (AIA, Prudential). Source: S51, S52.

**Pharmacy chains** — **Boots TH** app (500K+ downloads; free in-app pharmacist chat; coupons, loyalty). **Watsons TH** (1M+ downloads; BMI/BMR calculator, AI skin analysis, member card). **Pure** — no dedicated app details found (**unverified**). Sources: S53–S55.

**Home lab / care navigation** — **MedEx**: 3,000+ biomarkers, home service 750 THB nationwide; **HealthDeliver**: nurse visit 600 THB in Bangkok (700–1,500 elsewhere), tests from ~500 THB, results by secure email in 1–3 business days. "Healthserv" as a home-lab brand: **unverified** (healthserv.net appeared only as a news site). Source: S56, S57. (These are partner/supply options for RooSuk's precision-check business and sources of PDF lab reports to ingest.)

### 3.4 Insurer and bank apps

**AIA+ / AIA Vitality Thailand** — Points for screenings (750 pts for each of BMI, BP, glucose, cholesterol; +750 if in healthy range), up to 5,500 pts/year via online assessments, activity tracking via Health Connect/Apple Health, camera-based AI biometric scan (heart rate, stress, BMI), premium discounts and partner rewards; includes policy servicing. Strongest incumbent on gamification/wearables, but tied to policyholders. Sources: S58–S60.

**Allianz Ayudhya** — "myHealth by AllianzAyudhya" (benefits, claims, providers) being migrated into "My Allianz"; "My Health" platform and "Healthy Living" (employee wellbeing content, expert access, rewards). Discontinuation year **unverified**. Source: S61.

**Muang Thai Life — MTL Click** — Fit Rewards: log steps/exercise minutes, convert Fit Points into up to 15% renewal premium discount (eligible policies); mindfulness/nutrition/sleep content; Smile Club revamp. Source: S62.

**FWD Omne** — insurance + step challenges, health tracking, meditation, rewards. **FWD Group Health** app also exists. Source: S63.

**Banks (K PLUS, SCB EASY, Krungthai NEXT)** — no dedicated health/wellness features found; Krungthai's health relevance is via Paotang. They matter for **PromptPay**, not health. Source: S64.

### 3.5 Calorie / fitness / wellness / AI health startups

**Kalguroo** — Thai AI calorie tracker (launched Jul 2025): multimodal image analysis + RAG over a proprietary Thai food DB; seed round led by doctors/health-tech experts (amount undisclosed in snippets); 1M+ downloads in 152 countries, ~THB 20M ARR (target THB 100M), team of 8. Free 3 scans/day; subscriptions US$3.99–9.99/mo, US$59.99/yr. Roadmap: personalised AI coach on real user data, country-specific food DBs. Reviews: some Thai users say oils/basic ingredients missing and calories may be underestimated; non-Thai speakers note Thai-only food search. Biggest consumer-AI threat in Thailand for the "daily loop". Sources: S65–S68.

**NubCal** (Central Content Co.) — photo calorie counter, Apple Health integration, widgets, community; ~999 THB/yr; 130K+ downloads, ~4.83★. Source: S69.

**Fastcal** — Thai-tuned AI calorie app plus a Silom weight-loss clinic (doctor guidance through app); free with IAP (prices not seen). Source: S70.

**Gindee Food AI (CARIVA)** — LINE chatbot: send food photo or type menu -> calories/protein/fat/carbs + advice; clinician dashboard of eating history; claims 86% accuracy on Nutrition5k, 80,000+ user-corrected entries, 1M Thai food images. LINE-native (zero install) is a distribution lesson. Sources: S71, S72.

**Lab.morroo + Morroo Advice + drug.morroo (Morroo)** — Thai-first AI lab reader: photo/PDF/JPG -> extract values (WBC, Hb, FBS, creatinine…) -> flag abnormal -> Thai explanation -> save history/trend -> PDF export to doctor; free registration, no card, 3 free reads/month, "does not store images permanently". Morroo Advice: free 24h AI health consult in Thai; drug.morroo for pharmacists; Morroo EMR for clinics. Essentially RooSuk's lab feature as a standalone. Company size/traffic **unverified**. Sources: S73–S75.

**Kantesti and other foreign "AI blood test" apps** — upload PDF/JPG/PNG or type values; plain-language explanations in 75+ languages; meal/supplement plans (supplement recommendation with dosage raises claim/regulatory risk in Thailand). Thai App Store listing exists. Source: S76.

**MyFitnessPal / Cal AI / Lose It / YAZIO** — MyFitnessPal has a very large crowdsourced DB (variable Thai accuracy: e.g., "pad thai" entries 300–800 kcal per third-party review); Lose It/YAZIO have thin SE-Asian coverage; **Cal AI acquired by MyFitnessPal** (closed Dec 2025, announced 2 Mar 2026), 15M+ downloads, ~US$30M revenue, photo-first. Thai-language/Thai-food accuracy for Cal AI **unverified**. Sources: S77–S79.

**Strava** — added Thai in Mar 2026; top iOS Health & Fitness app in Thailand per Similarweb snippet; integrates with Apple Health. HealthifyMe Thailand presence: **unverified**. Sources: S80, S81.

## 5. Thai market facts (with sources)

1. **Digital fitness and well-being market:** Statista Market Insights projects Thailand's Digital Fitness & Well-Being revenue at **US$456.2M in 2025**, user penetration **22.65%**, ARPU about **US$27.99** (S88). A Thai marketing article summarising Statista cites digital treatment & care at about US$734M (+8%/yr) and digital fitness & wellbeing at about US$411M (+16.4%/yr) — different vintage/definition from the figure above; a "16.4% CAGR 2025–2035" for digital health also appeared in a snippet and its origin is **unverified** (S89).
2. **Wellness economy:** Global Wellness Institute: Thailand wellness market **US$42.7B in 2024** (US$38.8B in 2023, +10.1%), 24th largest globally; the prior year had grown 28.4% to US$40.5B; wellness tourism ~US$14B (S90, S91).
3. **PDPA:** health data is "sensitive" under PDPA Section 26 and needs **explicit, informed, specific consent clearly separate from other matters**; the PDPC issued its first major fines in **Aug 2025 (over THB 21.5M)**, six enforced cases since June 2022 (one in 2024, five in 2025); administrative fines up to THB 5M per violation (S92, S93). This supports RooSuk's separate consents for logs, photos, wearables and family sharing.
4. **Thai FDA — supplements and ads:** supplements are "food" under the Food Act B.E. 2522; claims to cure/treat/prevent/relieve disease are prohibited; Section 41 requires an advertisement permit before advertising food benefits in any medium including online; new **Food Advertising Criteria notification published 27 Jul 2026, effective 28 Jul 2026** (bans misleading claims, limits on models and doctor endorsements, restricted categories); enforcement reported to target registration holders and retailers (S94–S97). Relevant to RooSuk's marketplace and any AI-generated supplement suggestions.
5. **Thai FDA — software as medical device (SaMD)/AI:** guidance published Jun 2024, revised Oct 2024; software for diagnosis/prevention/monitoring/management (including clinical decision support and, e.g., mole-photo cancer-risk apps) may be a medical device; simple calculators (BMI), HIS/EHR that only store/display records and IVF calendar tools are not (S98). Supports RooSuk's "never diagnoses; explains and prepares for doctor" stance — legal classification of lab-interpretation AI still needs counsel (**unverified** for RooSuk's exact design).
6. **MOPH digital health policy:** National Digital Health Committee 5-year action plan 2566–2570 (B.E.; 2023–2027) with 5 strategies including governance, national digital health platform and ecosystem; PHR system; experts ranked the **Digital Health Act** as the top priority (not confirmed enacted in my sources) (S99). Mor Prom Super App is the flagship; Health Link links ~20,000 facilities (S13); MOPH–True MoU Mar 2026 on AI/consented-data health tips (S35).
7. **Smartphone, payments:** ~67.8M internet users (94.7% penetration), 99.5M mobile connections (139%), Android 67.7% vs iOS 32.1% of devices (S100). **PromptPay: 90M+ registrations (Jun 2025), ~74M transactions/day, 2.16B transactions worth THB 4.2T in Sep 2025**; 165.3M internet/mobile-banking accounts (Sep 2025) (S101).
8. **Ageing society:** Thailand is an aged society (~13.5M aged 60+, about 20%+ of population in 2025) and projected to become "super-aged" (28%+) around 2033–2034 per NESDC-based reports — drives demand for family sharing / caregiver features (S102).

## 6. Strategic observations for RooSuk (analysis, not sourced facts)

- **Do not lead with the food scanner** — Kalguroo/NubCal/Fastcal/HDmall/Gindee already serve it, and Kalguroo has a head start (1M+ downloads, ~4.8★). Lead with the _connected_ story: food + labs + wearables + documents + doctor brief, with granular PDPA consent.
- **HDmall is the most plausible direct entrant** into RooSuk's space (it already holds food scan + AI lab reading + chatbot + marketplace) but is a commerce funnel. RooSuk's counter-position is neutrality: no package-selling incentive, user-owned vault.
- **Lab.morroo proves demand and sets a low floor** (3 free reads/month, Thai). RooSuk's free-lite quota should be at least comparable.
- **Free national alternative exists** (Mor Prom, Paotang) for records and appointments; RooSuk should complement (import/export, Health Passport) rather than replicate.
- **Hospital apps have low-to-mixed ratings and are single-provider** (Siriraj 3.2★) — cross-provider aggregation is a real gap, but needs ingest via PDF/photo (no hospital API).
- **Gamification precedents tie rewards to steps/insurer discounts**; RooSuk's "reward showing up, not weight" is distinct and fits the Thai FDA/medical-claims caution.
- **Corporate:** Ooca/Doctor Anywhere show employers buy mental-health and teleconsult; an employer-paid daily-habit + checkup-records product is thinner.
- **Pricing check:** 49/89 THB/month is far below Kalguroo (US$3.99–9.99/mo) and NubCal (~999 THB/yr ≈ 83 THB/mo) on a per-feature basis; Gold 49 THB is near NubCal's effective monthly price.
